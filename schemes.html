How much assistance could apply?

The published PM Surya Ghar CFA structure provides 60% CFA for the first 2 kW and 40% of additional system cost for the next 1 kW, with the CFA capped at 3 kW.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is a central government rooftop solar initiative for eligible residential consumers.

Under the published CFA structure, the amount is ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or higher eligible residential systems.

PM-KUSUM is a renewable energy programme supporting solar applications in agriculture, including solar pumps and pump solarisation.

For PM Surya Ghar, use the official PM Surya Ghar National Portal. PM-KUSUM applications depend on the relevant component and designated state implementing authority.
